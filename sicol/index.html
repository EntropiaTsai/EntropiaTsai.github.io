🗣️ Script to read: "Now, let me show you an example of how we find these metaphors. Look at the sentence: 'Pan-blue legislators fled, tucking their tails'. Following the Conceptual Metaphor Domain Annotation Guidelines-CMDAG protocol—we first identify the literal Tenor 'Legislators' and the non-literal action 'Tucking Tail'. Then, based on the Ground of fleeing in defeat, we map them into the Target Domain 'Politician' and Source Domain 'Dog'. Finally, we push them into the macro frame of 'Politics' and 'Animal'. As you can see, this method captures not just nouns, but also dynamic actions.

To make the LLM execute this workflow, we designed two prompts. The first one extracts the tenor, vehicle, and ground. This output is then passed to the second prompt, which analyses the target and source domains."

  • Constraints：嚴格限制目標必須是政治，來源必須是具象實體。

  • ※ 防禦準備 (Q&A)：為何強調 Embodied domain？
    答：這呼應 CMT 的核心概念 (Embodiment)，隱喻必須建構在具體的身體或物理經驗上。在我們的 Prompt 中，我們明確要求 LLM 找的來源域必須是「可感知來源域」，並舉例：動物、戰爭、廢棄物、可操控的物件等。如果 LLM 抓出的 Source 也是抽象概念（例如：「這場選舉是一個『過程』」），或者缺乏具體物理互動（例如：「人民的力量」），就不算 embodied domain，在人工審查時會被剔除。
🗣️ Script to read: "More specifically, our prompts were designed with four key features. First, we gave the LLM a specific Persona: acting as both a metaphor linguist and a Taiwan politics expert. Then, we set strict Constraints: the Target must be political, and the Source must be an embodied domain. Next, we used a Chain of Thought approach. The most critical rule here is to judge the overall semantic frame before looking at literal words. Lastly, we provided Few-shot examples to anchor these abstract rules."

🗣️ Script to read: "Following the Chain of Thought mentioned earlier, this is what the actual reasoning chain looks like. The first prompt is the extraction flow. The model acts like a linguist to break down the sentence step by step. The LLM is asked to read the whole text to understand the context. Afterwards, it sequentially extracts the vehicle, the tenor, and the ground. The final step is to validate the output. It compares the extracted information with the context to confirm if the output is coherent or not.

Then, in the second prompt, the model acts like an analyst. It also reads the context and then performs 'Literal Restoration' to group the slang words into hypernymic categories like 'Animal' or 'War'."

The Jaccard similarity score was about 0.485. According to previous studies, this is actually a very strong performance for such an open-ended task. Second, to secure the accuracy for our downstream analysis, we manually reviewed the entire dataset and removed 129 incorrect mappings."

🗣️ Script to read: "However, when we zoom into the 'Animal' category, we see a massive shift. In 2014, the top three metaphors were 'Monkey', 'Insect', and 'Dog'. But in 2026, we found a huge change. First, 'Bird' increased to nearly 50 percent, reflecting the recent political incident known as the 'Bluebird Action'. Second, 'Livestock' emerged as a new popular insult. It should be noted that the word 'livestock' might seem like a neutral agricultural term in English. But in the context of Mandarin, it is a severe insult. It literally implies someone is 'not human'—degrading people from the opposing camp as lower-level beasts. Interestingly, 'Dog' is the only animal that remains stable across these twelve years. This proves that some metaphors remain stable over time, but others rapidly evolve alongside real-world events."

🗣️ Script to read: "Now, these findings show the power of our LLM pipeline, but getting these clean results required careful human review. Of course, the model is not perfect, and we noticed two main areas for improvement. First, the 'person-to-thing mismatch'. For example, when encountering a sentence like 'The Premier steps down from the stage'—which literally means stepping off a stage but actually means 'resigning'—the model sometimes mistakenly mapped a politician to a 'stage'. But as shown here, the source domain must match the ontological nature of the target. A politician should be mapped to a 'performer', or a political 'position' should be mapped to a 'stage'. Second, the 'unspecified vehicle'. The model sometimes put distinct animals into a generic 'Other' bucket, rather than specifying them as 'Monkey' or 'Bird'. This doesn't affect the macro-level analysis, but it blocks us from exploring finer details. We originally included the 'Other' option in our prompts to avoid missing metaphors, so this is a clear trade-off we need to balance in future research."

Looking forward

Will LLMs replace domain experts?

Language is alive and dynamic
  • Language evolves with personal style, culture, and time.
  • Static training data cannot fully capture this moving target.
The true role of linguists
  • Move beyond chasing the last few percentages of accuracy.
  • Guide AI to uncover the cultural logic behind massive texts.
  • 呼應主題：語言是活的，所以單靠靜態訓練資料去追逐 100% 準確率是不切實際的。

🗣️ Script to read: "Finally, will LLMs completely replace us? The answer is no. Language is alive, and it is always changing with culture. Static training data cannot fully capture this. Therefore, we should not blindly chase 100 percent accuracy. Instead, the true role of linguists is to guide AI to uncover the deep cultural logic behind the text."
